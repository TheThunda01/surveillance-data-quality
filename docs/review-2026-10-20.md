## Scope

Reviewed the README, contribution guidance, all authored guides, data dictionaries, diagnostic examples, source claims, and the three generated reports against the relevant code, frozen inputs, and cited sources. ## Corrections made

- Defined technical vocabulary in context and in a shared [glossary](glossary.md), including positivity, prevalence, denominator, percentage points, NAAT, reporting vintage, provenance, schema, preflight, and exit codes.
- Added an [assumption register](assumptions.md) with rationale, available checks, untested conditions, and consequences if a condition fails.
- Corrected the claim that the source-assigned surveillance week specifies when individual testing occurred. The extract has an aggregate reference week, not individual sample or test dates.
- Made the adapter's interpretation of `posted` explicit. Captured field names and calendar types do not provide its complete semantic definition, so it cannot establish patient-level delays or first public availability.
- Removed unsupported HMPV A/B detail. The selected source rows provide combined categories and do not support type-specific conclusions.
- Clarified that CSV row locators use a header-plus-record index and can differ from physical line numbers for multiline records. Documented the small numerical margin around the percentage-rounding boundary.
- Standardized citations to author–year links and added a [complete reference list](references.md). Verified publication metadata, added German's PMID and quiz pagination, Hohman's E43 identifier, and Silk's issue number. Removed an unnecessary exact publication-day claim for Mellor.
- Pinned the legacy Delphi documentation link to the same inspected commit as the V5 documentation. Distinguished source evidence from local design choices and software checks throughout.
- Kept the README, practical guide, and literature review aligned with the shared section order. Added cross-links so a reader can reach assumptions, definitions, citations, and access limits from the README.

## Source verification

The [reference audit](reference-audit.json) records 14 supporting sources. Five are publications or guidance reports: German (2001), Hohman (2024), Tang (2025), and Mellor and Silk (2026). Their metadata and relevant source text were checked. The four recent papers' complete PMC bodies were retrieved, with relevant methods, definitions, and limitations inspected. German's official CDC text was inspected. The remaining sources are the CDC dataset, three additional CDC pages, WHO methods, RFC 9457, and three pinned Delphi files.

Direct retrieval of the current dataset page displayed only a shell; indexed official text confirmed the missing-value notation. The live CDC metadata endpoint was unavailable through the retrieval tool. The frozen CSV, manifest, and compact metadata were inspected locally and checked by the existing loader. This verifies internal consistency, not independent source authenticity or a complete definition of `posted`.

Supplements, every reference cited by each paper, and sources excluded from the earlier searches were not comprehensively reviewed. The earlier unsuccessful Frictionless lookup remains an excluded historical record, not evidence for a claim. The original search logs retain their original retrieval and truncation details; the literature review separately describes this pass's additional access.
