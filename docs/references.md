# References and source roles

The citations below support particular definitions or design decisions. They do not certify the software. Author–year links in the documentation point to the relevant primary source. Access date: **October 20, 2026**. See the [reference audit](reference-audit.json) for verification detail and access limits.

## Research papers and guidance

- German, R. R., Lee, L. M., Horan, J. M., Milstein, R. L., Pertowski, C. A., Waller, M. N., and the Guidelines Working Group. (2001). Updated guidelines for evaluating public health surveillance systems: recommendations from the Guidelines Working Group. *MMWR Recommendations and Reports, 50*(RR-13), 1–35; quiz CE1–7. [PubMed, PMID 18634202](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18634202/); [official CDC full text](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/rr5013a1.htm). **Role:** completeness, validity, and timeliness are distinct evaluation concepts. No universal delay threshold is inferred.
- Hohman, K. H., Klompas, M., Zambarano, B., Wall, H. K., Jackson, S. L., and Kraus, E. M. (2024). Validation of Multi-State EHR-Based Network for Disease Surveillance (MENDS) Data and Implications for Improving Data Quality and Representativeness. *Preventing Chronic Disease, 21*, E43. [DOI: 10.5888/pcd21.230409](https://doi.org/10.5888/pcd21.230409); [PubMed, PMID 38870031](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38870031/). **Role:** examples of source and transformation problems in electronic health record (EHR) surveillance. Its setting differs from this aggregate laboratory extract.
- Mellor, J., Tang, M., Paton, R. S., and Ward, T. (2026). Recommendations for the analysis of leading indicators in epidemic surveillance. *PLOS Digital Health, 5*(10), e0001759. [DOI: 10.1371/journal.pdig.0001759](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pdig.0001759); [PubMed, PMID 42832413](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42832413/). **Role:** narrative methodological guidance about coverage, delays, revisions, and retrospective interpretation. It does not validate this project or supply a delay model.
- Silk, B. J., Prill, M. M., Winn, A. K., et al. (2026). Respiratory Virus Activity — United States, July 1, 2024–June 30, 2025. *MMWR Morbidity and Mortality Weekly Report, 75*(6), 77–84. [DOI: 10.15585/mmwr.mm7506a2](https://doi.org/10.15585/mmwr.mm7506a2); [PubMed, PMID 41712508](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41712508/). **Role:** primary surveillance context and NREVSS participation limits. The article covers a different period; its NREVSS results are not reproduced here.
- Tang, J., Rumack, A., Wilder, B., and Rosenfeld, R. (2025). Real-time forecasting of data revisions in epidemic surveillance streams. *PLOS Computational Biology, 21*(11), e1013709. [DOI: 10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013709](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013709); [PubMed, PMID 41264651](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41264651/). **Role:** evidence that numerator and denominator revisions can differ. Its revision-forecasting models and performance findings are not imported into this project.

## Public data and official documentation

- Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. (n.d.). *Percent positivity of respiratory viruses by nucleic acid amplification tests (NAATs), as reported on the National Respiratory and Enteric Virus Surveillance System (NREVSS) Dashboard* [dataset]. Identifier `rgnm-fkqb`; repository capture October 20, 2026. [Dataset](https://data.cdc.gov/Laboratory-Surveillance/Percent-positivity-of-respiratory-viruses-by-nucle/rgnm-fkqb); [pinned manifest](../data/public/manifest.json); [captured column metadata](../data/public/source_metadata.json). **Role:** actual input records and missing-value notation. The capture is a versioned extract, not a separate CDC release identifier. Current web rendering and metadata endpoint access were incomplete during review.
- Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. (2025a, June 18). *The National Respiratory and Enteric Virus Surveillance System (NREVSS)* [official overview]. [CDC page](https://www.cdc.gov/nrevss/php/dashboard/index.html). **Role:** voluntary weekly laboratory reporting, collection-week context, and viral categories. This overview does not fully define the extract's `posted` field.
- Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. (2025b, August 18). *Testing and Respiratory Viruses* [official information page]. [CDC page](https://www.cdc.gov/respiratory-viruses/prevention/testing.html). **Role:** distinction between nucleic acid amplification and antigen tests. It is not evidence of assay performance in these records.
- Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. (n.d.). *Use of Agency Materials* [official reuse policy]. [CDC page](https://www.cdc.gov/other/agencymaterials.html). **Role:** agency-material reuse, exceptions, attribution, and non-endorsement. The captured dataset metadata have no dataset-specific license identifier; this is not a new license grant.
- World Health Organization. (n.d.). *WHO COVID-19 dashboard: circulation* [official methods and interpretation notes]. [WHO page](https://data.who.int/dashboards/covid19/circulation). **Role:** positive/processed-test denominator logic and limits from testing and reporting practices. No WHO observations are used here.
