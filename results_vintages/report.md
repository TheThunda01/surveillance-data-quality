# Synthetic report-vintage analysis

As of 2026-10-20, with a 7-day grace period, 8 of 9 due slots have usable reports.

This is an invented version-history exercise. It is separate from the real CDC snapshot. A report issued after the cutoff is excluded. A revision replaces an older report; it is not another batch of tests. Negative revision deltas are permitted corrections, not negative test counts.

| As of | Due slots | Usable | Unreported | Future versions excluded | Positives / tests |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 2026-10-05 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 9 | 2 / 45 |
| 2026-10-12 | 6 | 6 | 0 | 4 | 10 / 109 |
| 2026-10-20 | 9 | 8 | 1 | 0 | 12 / 157 |

These denominators differ because different weeks are due and different revisions were available. Comparing these aggregate percentages as disease trends would be misleading.
